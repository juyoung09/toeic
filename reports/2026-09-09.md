- B. The cafeteria will be closed during renovations.
- C. Several employees requested new parking spaces.
- D. The invoice was sent last week.

Focus: visitor policy, register, security badge

## Answer Key

1. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

2. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

3. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

4. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

5. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

6. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

7. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

8. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.
