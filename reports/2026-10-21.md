## Answer Key

1. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

2. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

3. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

6. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

7. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

8. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.
