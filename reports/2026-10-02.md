- A. a
- B. an
- C. the
- D. no article

## Answer Key

1. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

2. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

3. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

6. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

7. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

8. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.
