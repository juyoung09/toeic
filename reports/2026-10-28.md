## Answer Key

1. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

2. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

3. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

4. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

5. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

6. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

7. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

8. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.
