## Answer Key

1. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

2. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

3. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

4. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

5. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

6. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

7. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

8. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.
