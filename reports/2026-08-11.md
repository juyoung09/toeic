## Answer Key

1. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

2. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

3. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

4. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

5. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

6. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

7. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

8. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.
