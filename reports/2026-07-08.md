## Answer Key

1. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

2. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

3. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

4. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

5. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

6. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

7. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

8. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.
