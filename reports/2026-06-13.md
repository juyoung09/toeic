## Answer Key

1. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

2. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

3. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

4. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

5. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

6. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

7. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

8. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.
