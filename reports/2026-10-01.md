## Answer Key

1. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

2. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

3. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

4. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

5. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

6. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

7. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

8. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.
