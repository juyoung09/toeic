## Answer Key

1. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

2. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

3. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

4. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

5. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

6. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

7. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

8. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.
