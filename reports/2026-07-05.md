- B. The cafeteria will be closed during renovations.
- C. Several employees requested new parking spaces.
- D. The invoice was sent last week.

Focus: visitor policy, register, security badge

## Answer Key

1. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

2. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

3. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

6. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

7. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

8. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.
