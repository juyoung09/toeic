## Answer Key

1. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

2. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

3. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

6. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

7. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

8. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.
