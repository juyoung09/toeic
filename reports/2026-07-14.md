- B. to

- D. with

## Answer Key

1. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

2. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

3. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

4. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

5. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

6. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

7. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

8. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.
