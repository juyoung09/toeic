### 8. Part 7 - inference

Notice: The 3 P.M. product demonstration has been moved from Room 204 to the main auditorium because attendance is higher than expected. What can be inferred?

- A. The demonstration was canceled.
- B. More people plan to attend than originally anticipated.
- C. Room 204 is being renovated.
- D. The event will begin earlier.

Focus: demonstration, auditorium, attendance

## Answer Key

1. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

2. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

3. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

4. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

5. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

6. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

7. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

8. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.
