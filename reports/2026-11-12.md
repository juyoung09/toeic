## Answer Key

1. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

2. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

3. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

6. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

7. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

8. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.
