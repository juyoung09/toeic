## Answer Key

1. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

2. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

3. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

4. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

5. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

6. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

7. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

8. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.
