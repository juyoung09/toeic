## Answer Key

1. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

2. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

3. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

4. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

5. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

6. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

7. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

8. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.
