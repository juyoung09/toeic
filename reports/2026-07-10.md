## Answer Key

1. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

2. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

3. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

4. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

5. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

6. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

7. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

8. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.
