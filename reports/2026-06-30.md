## Answer Key

1. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

2. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

3. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

4. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

5. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

6. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

7. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

8. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.
