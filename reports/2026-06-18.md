## Answer Key

1. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

2. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

3. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

4. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

5. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

6. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

7. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

8. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.
