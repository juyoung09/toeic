### 8. Part 5 - conjunctions

The seminar was postponed ___ the speaker had a scheduling conflict.

- A. because
- B. although
- C. unless
- D. so that

Focus: postpone, speaker, scheduling conflict

## Answer Key

1. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

2. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

3. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

4. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

5. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

6. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

7. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

8. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.
