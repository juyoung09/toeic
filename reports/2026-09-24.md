## Answer Key

1. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

2. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

3. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

4. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

5. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

6. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

7. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

8. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.
