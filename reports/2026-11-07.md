## Answer Key

1. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

2. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

3. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

4. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

5. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

6. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

7. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

8. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.
