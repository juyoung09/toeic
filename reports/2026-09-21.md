## Answer Key

1. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

2. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

3. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

4. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

5. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

6. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

7. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

8. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.
