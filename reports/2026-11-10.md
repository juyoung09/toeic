## Answer Key

1. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

2. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

3. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

4. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

5. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

6. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

7. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

8. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.
