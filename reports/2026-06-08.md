Memo: We have updated the visitor policy. Employees must register guests at least 24 hours before arrival. ___ Security badges will be prepared in advance.

- A. This will allow reception staff to confirm each appointment.
- B. The cafeteria will be closed during renovations.
- C. Several employees requested new parking spaces.
- D. The invoice was sent last week.

Focus: visitor policy, register, security badge

## Answer Key

1. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

2. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

3. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

4. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

5. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

6. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

7. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

8. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.
