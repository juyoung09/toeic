## Answer Key

1. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

2. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

3. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

4. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

5. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

6. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

7. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

8. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.
