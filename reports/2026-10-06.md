## Answer Key

1. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

2. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

3. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

4. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

5. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.

6. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

7. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

8. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.
