## Answer Key

1. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

2. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.

3. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

4. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

5. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

6. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

7. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

8. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.
