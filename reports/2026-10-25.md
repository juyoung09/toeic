## Answer Key

1. B - 'Look over' means to review or inspect something briefly.

2. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

3. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

4. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

5. B - 'Additional' starts with a vowel sound, so 'an' is used before it.

6. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

7. B - The room changed because expected attendance increased.

8. C - The subject is singular: 'the list', not 'vendors'.
