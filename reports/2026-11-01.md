## Answer Key

1. B - Future perfect passive fits an action completed before a future time.

2. C - 'Who' is the subject of the relative clause referring to a person.

3. A - 'Launch' means to start or introduce a new program or product.

4. A - The second clause gives the reason for the postponement.

5. A - Choice A connects visitor registration with reception and security preparation.

6. B - Use 'more + adjective + than' for comparative forms of many longer adjectives.

7. B - ask + someone + to do something is the correct structure.

8. C - An adverb is needed to modify the verb phrase 'will be installed'.
